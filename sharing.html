The First Lesson I Learned About Depending on Others Life Lesson

When I was in 5th class, one of my friends had recently shifted from Telugu medium to English medium. He came to me before our examinations and said, “You are good at Mathematics. Help me in the Mathematics exam, and I will help you in Telugu.”

At that time, I was good at Mathematics, but Telugu was not my strongest subject. So, I trusted him and accepted his deal. Our first exam was Mathematics. During the exam, I helped him whenever he needed me. I thought that when the Telugu exam came, he would do the same for me.

Then came the Telugu exam. While I was writing the exam, I tried to get his attention and asked him to show me the answers. But he stayed completely quiet. He didn't even turn around to look at me.

At that moment, I understood something. I realized that I had made a mistake by entering an examination depending on someone else. That was the first—and last—time in my life that I depended on another person to help me during an exam.

I didn't take that experience as a reason to hate him. Instead, I took it as a lesson. From that day until today, I have never depended on someone else to show me answers in an examination. I learned to trust my own preparation, my own knowledge, and my own ability.

"It may have happened when I was only in 5th class, but the lesson stayed with me for years: Never depend on someone else for something that determines your own success."
